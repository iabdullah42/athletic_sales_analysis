# Athletic Sales Analysis (2020–2021)

Combines two years of athletic-footwear and apparel sales data and answers business questions with pandas.

**Questions answered**
- Which region sold the most products, and which region had the most sales revenue?
- Which retailer had the most sales, and in which region and city?
- Which retailers led women's athletic footwear sales?
- Which days and weeks had the highest women's footwear sales?

**Techniques:** concatenating and cleaning multi-year data, `groupby`, `pivot_table`, date handling, and resampling to daily and weekly totals

**Notebook:** `athletic_sales_analysis_starter_code.ipynb` (data in `Resources/`)

**Tools:** Python, pandas
